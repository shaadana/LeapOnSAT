# LeapOn Documentation

This document explains how to operate the LeapOn platform. It is written for the person using the platform. Each section begins with the onboarding sequence in the order it occurs, then describes the features available to that user type, and ends with troubleshooting steps.

---

## Getting an Account

LeapOn does not send invitation emails. You create your own account through the standard sign-up screen.

1. Open the application URL. If you are not logged in, the home page loads the public landing page. Select the sign-in or sign-up control to open the login screen.
2. Register with your email and password, or sign in if you already have an account.
3. After you log in, the platform checks whether your account has a role assigned. If it does not, you are redirected to the Onboarding page. You cannot reach the main application until you select a role.

If you see a "User not registered" message, your account exists in the login system but has not been added to this application. Contact the platform administrator to have your account added.

## Student Portal

### Onboarding Sequence

The steps below occur in order the first time you log in as a student.

1. **Select your role.** On the Onboarding page, three cards are shown. Select "I'm a Student." The platform records the student role on your account.
2. **Enroll in your classes.** The first slide lists two classes that you are enrolled in automatically ("SAT MATH" and "LeapOn Test Teacher"). Select "Enroll and Continue." The platform adds you to these classes. If enrollment fails, you can join classes later from the My Groups page.
3. **Enter your profile details.** The second slide asks for your grade level, target SAT date (or select "I don't have a date yet"), current SAT Math and English scores if you have them, and your goal scores. You can also enable daily study reminders and confirm the email address reminders are sent to. Select "Save and Continue."
4. **Set reward preferences.** The third slide lets you enable or disable rewards (XP, leveling, the Avatar Shop, and the Memory Palace). Select "Save and Continue." You can change this later in Settings.
5. **Start the SAT Math diagnostic.** The final slide recommends the diagnostic. Select "Start Diagnostic Now" to go to the SAT Math Diagnostic page. Completing the diagnostic calibrates your recommended practice. If you leave onboarding without completing it, you can start it later from the SAT Practice menu.

After the slideshow, the Dashboard loads.

### Features

#### Dashboard
The Dashboard is your default landing page. It shows your Daily Study Plan, recommended tasks, active assignments, and recent activity in a single feed, with your study streak and daily practice goal shown in a side column. Recommendations prioritize completing diagnostics first, then daily practice needs based on your knowledge graph and performance.

#### Learner's Profile
The Learner's Profile is a self-assessment, separate from the SAT subject diagnostics. It contains three sections: Executive Functioning, Mindset, and Motivation. Each section takes a few minutes. After all three are complete, the platform saves your results to your profile, identifies your top three strengths and top three growth areas, and shows a results summary. You can retake it from this page at any time. The assessment takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes total.

#### SAT Math Practice
Open SAT Math Practice from the SAT Practice menu to start a configurable practice session. You choose a session type, the math domains to cover, the difficulty, and where the questions come from. Sessions adapt difficulty based on your answers.

#### SAT Math Diagnostic
The SAT Math Diagnostic establishes your baseline mastery across all math domains. It is the diagnostic referenced during onboarding. You can retake it from the SAT Practice menu to recalibrate your levels.

#### SAT Math Module Test
The Module Test simulates a full SAT Math module under timed testing conditions.

#### SAT Math Knowledge Graph
The Math Knowledge Graph is a visual map of SAT math concepts with your current mastery level for each.

#### SAT English Practice
Open SAT English Practice from the SAT Practice menu. It presents category cards for grammar, vocabulary, and reading practice. Select a category to begin a session.

#### SAT English Diagnostic
The SAT English Diagnostic establishes your baseline for grammar, vocabulary, and reading comprehension. You can retake it from the SAT Practice menu.

#### SAT English Practice Test
The Practice Test runs a full SAT English test using two 27-question modules with adaptive difficulty between them. A calculator panel and scratchpad are available. Answer checking is disabled during the test to match real testing conditions. Scoring is calibrated to 10-point increments.

#### English Knowledge Graph
The English Knowledge Graph is a visual map of SAT English skills with your current mastery level for each.

#### Deep Review
Deep Review shows questions you have previously missed so you can re-attempt them. You can review by session or by domain, and you can revisit bookmarked questions here.

#### Independent Study
Independent Study provides lesson content organized by domain and subtopic. Lessons include instruction and practice problems. Completed lessons are recorded on your profile.

#### LEAP Pathways
LEAP Pathways (Study Habits) lets you select and maintain learning habits and pathways. Active habits feed into your Daily Study Plan and streak tracking.

#### Study Streak
Study Streak tracks consecutive days of practice. If daily reminders are enabled, you receive an email nudge. Streak freezes are available based on your preferences.

#### Study Calendar
The Study Calendar shows your study sessions, class events, and scheduled assignments. You can respond to class calendar events from here.

#### My Coach
My Coach is a conversational assistant that has access to your performance data. Ask it about concepts, request study recommendations, or plan a session.

#### My Groups
My Groups lists the classes and families you belong to. Select "View Class" or "View Family" to open the group page. Group pages show announcements, assignments, chat, and shared media. Select "Join Another Group" to add a new class or family.

#### Joining a Class or Family
1. Open My Groups and select "Join a Group" or "Join Another Group."
2. Choose whether you are joining a class or a family.
3. Enter the six-character join code you received from your teacher or parent, or select a class from the list of joinable classes. Codes are not case sensitive because the field uppercases input automatically.
4. Select "Join." You are redirected to My Groups, where the new group appears.

#### Avatar Shop and Memory Palace
If rewards are enabled, the Avatar Shop lets you spend earned coins on avatar customizations. The Memory Palace is a spatial recall tool where you organize notes into rooms and practice recalling them.

#### Settings
Settings let you change your display name, timezone, grade level, target SAT date, rewards toggle, and email notification toggle. You can replay the quick tour from here. The Danger Zone contains the Delete Account action.

### Troubleshooting

- **I cannot get past the Onboarding page.** You must select a role card to continue. If the page does not advance after selecting "I'm a Student," refresh the page and try again.
- **My classes did not enroll during onboarding.** Open My Groups and select "Join a Group," then join the class using its join code.
- **My Dashboard shows no recommended tasks.** Complete the SAT Math Diagnostic and the SAT English Diagnostic. The recommendation engine requires diagnostic results to generate tasks.
- **A join code is rejected.** Confirm the code with the teacher or parent who gave it to you. The code must be six characters. If the group still does not appear, return to My Groups and try again.
- **A practice session loads no questions.** The combination of domain and difficulty you selected may have no questions available. Widen the difficulty selection or add more domains.
- **My streak did not increase after practicing.** Confirm the session was completed, not abandoned. Check your daily practice goal in Settings.
- **My free-response math answer is marked wrong.** Verify your answer formatting. Free-response answers are sensitive to decimal and fraction notation.

## Parent Portal

### Onboarding Sequence

1. **Select your role.** On the Onboarding page, select "I'm a Parent." The platform records the parent role and takes you directly to the Parent Portal. There is no slideshow for parents.
2. **Watch the quick tour.** The first time you open the Parent Portal, a quick tour overlays the page. Step through it to learn where each feature is. You can replay it later from Settings.
3. **Create a family.** Open the Families tab and create a family. The platform generates a unique join code for the family.
4. **Connect your children.** Share the join code with each child. The child signs in with their own student account, opens My Groups, selects "Join a Group," chooses "Join a Family," and enters the code. Once a child joins, they appear in your family immediately.

### Features

The Parent Portal is organized into five tabs.

#### Families
The Families tab lists every family you own. Each family card shows the join code and the number of connected children. From here you can create a new family or delete an existing one. Selecting a family opens the Children List, which shows each child's proficiency, diagnostic accuracy, strengths, and growth areas. Select "View Full Profile" on a child to open the Student Profile View.

The Student Profile View contains tabs for Math, English, Assignments, and Insights. It displays the child's complete history, including practice and diagnostic data generated before the child was connected to your family. The data is retrieved through a backend function that verifies you own the family the child belongs to.

#### Goals
The Goals tab contains Household Goals and Family Events. Create academic or behavioral goals for the family, and schedule family events such as study sessions or test dates.

#### Coach
The Coach tab opens the Family Coach, a conversational assistant with access to your family's goals, events, and connected children's performance data. Use it for recommendations on supporting your child's learning.

#### Mentor
The Mentor tab contains the Parent Mentorship Helper, which provides guidance on supporting a student through SAT preparation, and the Parent Mindset Display, which shows your mindset profile if you have completed the parent mindset assessment.

#### Practice
The Practice tab presents roleplay scenarios you can use to rehearse conversations with your child about academics, motivation, and planning.

#### Settings
Settings let you change your display name, timezone, and email notification toggle, replay the quick tour, and delete your account.

### Troubleshooting

- **My child does not appear in the family.** Confirm the child entered the correct join code from their own account on the Join a Group page. The child must join from a student account, not a parent account. If the join succeeded but the child is missing, refresh the Families tab; the list updates in real time when a child joins.
- **I cannot open a child's full profile.** You must own the family the child belongs to. If the child belongs to a different parent's family, access is denied. Verify the child is listed under the correct family.
- **The child's profile shows no history.** The child may not have generated any practice or diagnostic data yet. Confirm with the child that they have completed a practice session or diagnostic. New connections with no history display empty states.
- **Family creation fails.** Confirm the family name field is completed. If the error persists, sign out and sign back in, then try again.

## Teacher Portal

### Onboarding Sequence

1. **Select your role.** On the Onboarding page, select "I'm an Educator." The platform records the teacher role and takes you directly to the Teacher Portal. There is no slideshow for teachers.
2. **Watch the quick tour.** The first time you open the Teacher Portal, a quick tour overlays the page. Step through it to learn where each feature is. You can replay it later from Settings.
3. **Create a class.** Open the Classes tab and create a class. The platform generates a unique join code for the class.
4. **Enroll students.** Share the join code with your students. Each student signs in with their own account, opens My Groups, selects "Join a Group," chooses "Join a Class," and enters the code. Enrolled students appear on the class roster immediately.
5. **Complete your teacher profile (optional).** Open My Profile from the navigation to complete the Teacher Profile diagnostic. This is a multi-section assessment of teaching background, mindset, and practices. It is optional and not required to use the portal.

### Features

The Teacher Portal is organized into sections accessible from the navigation menu. Each section is shown one at a time.

#### Classes
The Classes section lists every class you own. Select a class to open its page, which contains the student roster, announcements, a class chat, a shared media library, and a class calendar. From the roster you can open any student's full performance view, which shows their SAT Math and English practice, diagnostic results, domain mastery, assignment progress, and session history.

#### Assignments
The Assignments section is where you create and manage assignments for your classes. Available assignment types are SAT practice, English practice, independent study, diagnostic, English diagnostic, supplemental diagnostic, lesson, document markup, auto-extract, and Canyon PDF. For each assignment you select the class, configure the options relevant to the type (domains, difficulty, duration, question source, allowed tools, lesson subject and subtopic, or supplemental level), and set a due date. You can schedule an assignment to post at a future date and time. Student progress is tracked per assignment, and you can view results from the assignment's results view.

#### Calendar
The Calendar section shows your scheduled assignments and class events across all of your classes.

#### Custom Rewards
The Custom Rewards section lets you define rewards for a class. Students redeem rewards using coins they earn through the rewards system.

#### Session Tools
Session Tools contains the Session Summary Manager. Use it to create a weekly summary for a student, including the focus topic, what the student did well, the mistake pattern, a tutor note, and the next step. You can email a summary to a parent. You can also push a session review form to a student, which asks them to rate the session on five measures.

#### Message Generator
The Message Generator produces draft messages to parents or students. Provide the context, generate the draft, review it, and send it.

#### Practice Scenarios
Practice Scenarios presents roleplay situations for rehearsing instructional and motivational conversations.

#### Co-Mentor
Co-Mentor is a conversational assistant with access to your class and student data. Use it for instructional guidance and student analysis. Conversations you remove from the list are hidden, not deleted, and can be restored by an administrator.

#### My Profile
My Profile contains the Teacher Profile diagnostic and its results, including mindset scores, teaching practice tendencies, strengths, growth areas, and personalized advice.

#### Settings
Settings let you change your display name and timezone, toggle email notifications, replay the quick tour, and delete your account.

### Troubleshooting

- **Students cannot join my class.** Confirm the join code shown on the class page matches the code you gave students. The student must join from their own student account on the Join a Group page. If a student joined but is not on the roster, refresh the class page; the roster updates in real time.
- **An assignment is not visible to students.** If you set a future post date, the assignment appears to students only at that date and time. Confirm the assignment status is active and that it is assigned to the correct class.
- **Assignment results show no data.** Results appear only after at least one student has completed the assignment. Check the assignment's progress view for per-student status.
- **A session summary email did not arrive.** Confirm the parent email address is correct. Email delivery to addresses that are not registered app users requires a connected custom domain on a paid plan and is subject to delivery limits. If delivery fails, verify the address and resend.
- **A Co-Mentor conversation disappeared.** Removed conversations are hidden, not deleted. To restore them, ask an administrator to clear the hidden conversation list on your teacher profile.
- **I cannot see a student's performance.** You must own the class the student is enrolled in. Students belonging to another teacher's class are not accessible to you.

## General Troubleshooting

- **I cannot sign in.** Confirm your email and password. Use the reset control on the login screen if you have forgotten your password.
- **"User not registered" appears after sign-in.** Your account exists in the login system but has not been added to this application. Contact the administrator to be added.
- **A page does not load.** Refresh the page. If it continues to fail, sign out and sign back in to refresh your session.
- **A list does not update after a change.** Refresh the page. Lists use real-time subscriptions that re-establish on load.
- **A record I should be able to see is hidden.** Access is restricted by ownership. Students see only their own data. Parents see only data for children in families they own. Teachers see only data for classes they own. If you need access outside these boundaries, the administrator must perform the action.

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Open Settings, scroll to the Danger Zone, and select "Delete Account." Confirm by typing DELETE. The platform deletes your profile, diagnostic results, study habits, practice sessions, and role-specific data (your classes and student associations for teachers, your families and goals for parents, your class and family memberships for students), then signs you out. App owners cannot be deleted; if your account is the app owner, the operation is blocked and you must transfer ownership first.
