### What We've Done for Underserved Communities and Our Plans: 

As LeapOn is currently completely free, with the entire platform, its resources, its AI integrations, and its lessons being available to users without free trials or subscriptions of any kind, we have already taken preliminary steps to prevent underserved communities from facing difficulties when attempting to access our platform. In addition, as LeapOn contains resources not only for SAT Math and English, but for life skills, executive functioning skills, and independent study ventures as well, we have already started to pave an avenue full of enrichment for those who would not have had the resources to seek it otherwise.
