### What We've Done for Underserved Communities and Our Plans: 

As LeapOn is currently completely free, with the entire platform, its resources, its AI integrations, and its lessons being available to users without free trials or subscriptions of any kind, we have already taken preliminary steps to prevent underserved communities from facing difficulties when attempting to access our platform. In addition, as LeapOn contains resources not only for SAT Math and English, but for life skills, executive functioning skills, and independent study ventures as well, we have already started to pave an avenue full of enrichment for those who would not have had the resources to seek it otherwise. 

### Broader Plans:
As LeapOn SAT is currently up and running for users of all ages and grade levels, we first seek to continue slowly adapting the platform based on student feedback and performance of current students on the upcoming SAT tests. As we refine agentic workflows, add gamification features, populate our databases with more questions, and generally enhance the quality of existing content, we will begin to expand into PSAT and pre-SAT skill-building courses and features aimed towards younger high schoolers and middle schoolers. While this will benefit all users, including those in communities where private tutors, SAT Prep academies, and other such pricy programs are readily paid for, we believe that underserved and marginalized communities will benefit especially from the closing of the knowledge gaps at earlier ages. 

LeapOn SAT's three-portal system, comprising portals for students, parents, and teachers that can all be linked to streamline communication and share progress in respectful, constructive ways, is something we will continue to emphasize and build upon as we onboard families and student groups, with the eventual goal being to roll use of the LeapOn SAT platform out to schools in communities across the state and, eventually, the nation.
